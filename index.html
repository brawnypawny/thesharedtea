ABOUT

a Flavor Lab

Where tapioca pearls meet smoothie swirls and classic teas get a lime twist, the bubble tea experiments of your dreams can exist! Swirls of colors come together as art in a cup -- our Flavor Lab is full of playful combinations waiting to be discovered.

Quality + Time

Drinks made for Quality Time using Quality Ingredients. Family-owned, we brew our tea from authentically-sourced leaves and hand-cut fresh fruits to bring out each texture profile. Spend your quality time with your people over a cup full of this stuff.

for Sharing

The sweetest moments are the shared ones. Whether it's catching up with friends, celebrating milestones, or making new connections, stop by in Brookline Village to make memories over delicious drinks.
